**Goal:** Apply everything from the first six modules to ship two real-shape projects you can talk about in an interview or a code review.

## Capstone A — AI Support-Bot Analytics  (`41_capstone_analytics.ipynb`)

A complete analytical study of an AI support deployment. **5 channels × 12 months × 8 KPIs** → exploration → 2×2 executive dashboard → regression that exposes Simpson's paradox → distance-matrix clustering → executive summary.

This is the *analytical* version of "shipping a project". The deliverable is a single document a manager could read in five minutes and take to a meeting.

## Capstone B — AI Customer-Feedback Assistant  (`42_capstone_ai_assistant.ipynb`)

A complete AI feature, end-to-end. Free-form feedback → LLM classification + extraction → grounded answers via RAG → orchestrated as a scheduled job with alerts + a cost dashboard.

This is the *engineering* version of "shipping a project". The deliverable is a small piece of software you could drop into a real product.

## How to use this module

- **If you have the time**, do both. They complement each other — A teaches the analytical mindset, B teaches the engineering mindset.
- **If you have to pick one**, pick the one that matches the job you want next.
- **In an interview**, talk through one of these as your "project I built". You wrote the code; you understand every part.
